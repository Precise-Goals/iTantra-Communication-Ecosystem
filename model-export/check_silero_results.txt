Interpretation:
  Without the 64-sample context, even a clearly-speech synthetic sample never reaches the 0.5
  threshold (max 0.259) — the missing context pushes output toward zero, not toward saturation
  at 1. With the context, the same audio correctly registers as speech in most frames while
  silence stays correctly near-zero either way. This confirms the T62 diagnosis and the fix's
  correctness on this specific (synthetic-voice) test.

Caveat: this validates the model/code against a synthesized voice, not a live human speaker.
Live human-voice confirmation on real hardware is a separate, additional check — see the
telemetry/logcat evidence committed alongside this file for that.
